sitefactory preset gallery

One preview per shipped theme preset: the factory demo home rendered with that preset's tokens, plus a figure specimen (real ImageRenderer output, ADR #84 figure tokens) and the block specimens (real composed output of every #128 block kind). Built by CI from ops/catalog/themes.yaml; order = catalog order. Previews are the home page only: their demo nav is inert (those pages are not part of a preview), and the catalog row / meta.yaml links open the snapshots bundled with this deploy.
